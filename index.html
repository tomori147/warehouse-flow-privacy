Warehouse Flow – Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game Warehouse Flow (Android package pl.smartcode.warehouseflow, iOS bundle pl.smartcode.warehouseclicker) collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

The game is published by Tomasz Marczak, who is the data controller. Contact: tomasz@smartcodetm.pl.

What we do not collect

  • The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads. They are served by Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID,
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is derived,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version) and how you interact with ads,
  • diagnostic data about ad loading.

Google uses this data to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. Personalised ads are shown only if you consent.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): on first launch the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Menu › Game › Privacy. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalised ads, and the game works the same.

See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The game uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to learn how players progress and where the game breaks. They collect:

  • a random app instance ID, created on install and not linked to your identity,
  • game events such as tutorial steps passed, upgrades bought, levels reached and rewarded ads watched,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, country, app version) and how the game was installed (store campaign),
  • crash reports and error logs.

This data is used only for analytics and fixing bugs. It is not used for advertising: ad-related signals are turned off by default in the game's analytics settings, and we never set a user ID.

Legal basis

  • Personalised advertising: your consent.
  • Non-personalised ads, analytics and crash reports: our legitimate interest in funding, improving and fixing a free game.

Who receives the data

Google acts as our service provider for AdMob and Firebase. Data may be processed outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We do not sell your data.

How long it is kept

  • Analytics data: up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Advertising data: as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices and rights

  • Change ad consent in Menu › Game › Privacy.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the game removes its save and its app instance ID.
  • Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. Because the game has no accounts, include your app instance ID or a description of your device when you write to us, so we can find the data.
  • You can complain to a data protection authority; in Poland that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
